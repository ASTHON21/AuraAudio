## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an introspective, serene, and ultra-refined listening sanctuary. Grounded in reductionist minimalism and elevated through nuanced glassmorphism, it treats sound as an intimate architectural space rather than a loud dashboard. 

The aesthetic marries pure precision with atmospheric tactility: deep abyssal dark tones, delicate spectral light highlights, hairline borders, and generous spatial voids. The experience is designed for discerning audiophiles, creatives, and design-conscious listeners who seek respite from the over-saturated, hyperactive interfaces of mainstream media platforms. Visual clutter is methodically removed; metadata, interactive controls, and acoustic wave feedback surface organically only when contextually relevant.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep tonal immersion punctuated by focused, luminous optical events:
- **Base Neutral (`#0D0E12`)**: A void-depth graphite-black canvas that absorbs visual noise and lets album artwork and optical glows emerge effortlessly.
- **Surface Elevation Levels**: Derived directly from the base neutral via semi-transparent layering (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), maintaining spectral depth rather than flat grey stepping.
- **Secondary (`#F4F5F7`)**: Aurora Cold Silver, acting as the primary high-legibility typographic anchor, dynamic icon color, and structural highlight.
- **Primary (`#4F75FF`)**: Dawn Azure, reserved exclusively for active playback states, live audio scrubbers, and critical primary interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#FFA877`)**: Warm Apricot, used sparingly as an organic counterpoint for warm accents, lossless/high-res audio badges, and intimate playback cues.
- **Borders & Separators**: Hairline alpha boundaries strictly set at `rgba(244, 245, 247, 0.08)` to define spatial edges without creating harsh silhouettes.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a balance between geometric clarity and humane curvature. The geometric construction gives tracks, metadata, and timestamps a technical purity, while its softened terminals prevent cold mechanical sterility.

- Track titles use tight optical tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor attention cleanly against artwork.
- Timestamps and scrub metrics use `body-sm` with tabular numerical figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure zero jitter during real-time playback.
- Sub-labels, audio bitrates (e.g., "96kHz / 24-bit FLAC"), and category tags utilize `label-sm` rendered in uppercase with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.08em`) for quiet, authoritative micro-hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 4px base vertical rhythm embedded into a fluid-column mobile framework. 

- **Canvas Safe Area & Margins**: Margins strictly isolate content from edge-bleed, employing `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports and scaling to `2rem` (32px) on tablet breakpoints.
- **Vertical Airflow**: Player views favor vertical breathing space over dense data packing. The distance between the album art canvas and track metadata relies on `space-xl`, allowing the visual art to resonate independently before typography introduces intellectual context.
- **Component Padding**: Micro-interactive controls maintain generous touch targets (minimum 44x44px bounding area) while maintaining compact visual envelopes utilizing `space-xs` and `space-sm` for internal paddings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is engineered through stacked optical refraction and controlled ambient translucency, avoiding heavy dropped shadows:

1. **Base Layer (Level 0)**: Unadorned `#0D0E12`, augmented in playback views by an organic, oversized 120px Gaussian blur extracted from the active album cover palette at 15% opacity.
2. **Glassmorphic Panes (Level 1 - Floating Cards & Drawers)**: 
   - Surface: `rgba(244, 245, 247, 0.04)`
   - Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`
   - Border: Hairline inner stroke `1px solid rgba(244, 245, 247, 0.08)`
3. **Modal Sheets & Tooltip Overlays (Level 2)**:
   - Surface: `rgba(13, 14, 18, 0.72)`
   - Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(36px)`
   - Rim Light: Top border highlight `1px solid rgba(244, 245, 247, 0.15)`
   - Shadow: `0 16px 40px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`
4. **Active Playback Elements (Glow Elevation)**: Playhead indicators and active state nodes cast a faint monochromatic aura using `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(79, 117, 255, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The system relies on high-curvature silhouettes that feel warm, ergonomic, and touch-sculpted:
- Small controls, micro chips, and playback toggles utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- Artwork tiles, feature guide floating cards, and drawer modals take on `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) to present fluid, friendly contours against dark fields.
- Micro badges and primary circular action toggles (play/pause, shuffle, airplay) implement full pill and circle radii (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons & Transport Controls
- **Primary Playback Anchor (Play/Pause)**: 64x64px circular button with `#F4F5F7` background, casting an internal micro-bevel. Dynamic icon glyph in `#0D0E12`.
- **Secondary Transports (Skip, Seek, Shuffle, Repeat)**: Minimalist 44x44px touch frames with borderless transparent surfaces. Glyphs sit at 50% opacity silver (`#F4F5F7`), shifting to 100% with a Dawn Azure glow point when active.

### Seekbar & Scrubber
- Hairline horizontal track of 3px height with `rgba(244, 245, 247, 0.12)` fill.
- Buffered segment illuminated via `rgba(244, 245, 247, 0.25)`.
- Active progress rendered in `#4F75FF` leading to an 8px polished white thumb that expands to 14px on active drag gesture.

### Chips & Badges
- Small metadata tags (e.g., "LOSSLESS", "DOLBY ATMOS", "HI-RES") use `label-sm` with 4px vertical and 8px horizontal padding.
- Neutral states have `rgba(244, 245, 247, 0.05)` fill with `1px solid rgba(244, 245, 247, 0.1)`. Highlight badges use warm apricot micro-gradients (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 168, 119, 0.15), rgba(79, 117, 255, 0.05))`).

### Floating Tutorial / Feature Guide Cards
- Compact floating cards anchored above the tracklist or playback view with `rounded-xl` contours.
- Backdrop frosted with 32px Gaussian blur and bounded by hairline perimeter lighting.
- Left-aligned micro-icon in a tinted dawn-blue enclosure, accompanied by succinct two-line value text and a subtle pill-shaped dismiss trigger.

### Input Fields & Search
- Recessed pill fields with `rgba(244, 245, 247, 0.05)` background and no static outline.
- Active typing invokes a smooth transition to `1px solid rgba(79, 117, 255, 0.4)` without hard drop shadows.

### Track Lists & Row Items
- Zero divider lines. Visual separation is maintained purely through vertical rhythm and micro-hover glass flashes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`).
- Leading element: 44x44px rounded album cover or track indexing number. Trailing element: Contextual three-dot overflow button at 40% resting opacity.
